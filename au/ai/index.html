About this setup

Dominate AI is everything in Scale plus an AI chat widget on your website and an AI voice agent for inbound calls.

Both answer from a knowledge base you'll build with us, so the AI never invents anything about your studio.

This form takes about 10 minutes. Straight after payment we scan your website and pre-fill your AI knowledge base for you — you confirm, tweak anything that isn't quite right, and you're done.

Your account typically goes live 7 to 10 business days after we review and approve the knowledge base.

📋
What you'll need before you start
  • Your studio logo as a PNG with a transparent background (350 × 180 px, max 2.5 MB)
  • Brand colour hex codes, or a screenshot we can match
  • The email address you want family replies to land in
  • An SMS number preference: a new Australian mobile we provide, or porting an existing one
  • Your studio's website URL — we scan it the moment you pay and pre-fill your AI knowledge base from it. No website yet? You can still add one on the next screen, or fill the knowledge base in by hand.
