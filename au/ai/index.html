About this setup

Dominate AI is everything in Scale plus an AI chat widget on your website and an AI voice agent for inbound calls.

Both answer from a knowledge base you'll build with us, so the AI never invents anything about your studio.

This form takes about 10 minutes. Straight after payment we scan your website and pre-fill your AI knowledge base for you, then you confirm it, tweak anything that isn't quite right, and you're done.

Your account typically goes live 7 to 10 business days after we review and approve the knowledge base.

📋

  • The email address you want family replies to land in
  • Whether you would like us to set up text messaging for you (we handle the number and carrier setup)
