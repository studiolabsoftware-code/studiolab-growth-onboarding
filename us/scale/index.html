About this setup

Scale takes the email automations from Launch and adds SMS, missed call text-back, and multi-channel lead source tracking. Every enquiry across Facebook, Google, your website, and your phone funnels into one structured nurture flow.

This form takes around 15 minutes to complete. We'll have your account ready within 3 to 7 business days of submission, depending on the setup option you choose below.

📋
What you'll need before you start
  • Your studio logo (PNG or SVG with a transparent background works best)
  • Brand colour hex codes, or a screenshot we can match
  • The email address you want family replies to land in
  • An SMS number preference: local, toll-free, or porting an existing one
  • Which lead sources to connect: Facebook Lead Ads, Google Business Messages, website chat
