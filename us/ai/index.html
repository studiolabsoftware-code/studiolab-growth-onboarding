About this setup

Dominate AI is everything in Scale plus an AI chat widget on your website and an AI voice agent for inbound calls.

Both answer from your own studio data, already in StudioLAB, so the AI never invents anything about your studio.

This form takes about 10 minutes and it saves as you go. There is nothing to fill in afterwards: your AI already knows your studio from your StudioLAB data.

Most studios are live within 7 to 10 business days, once your AI chat and voice agents are set up and tested.

📋

  • The email address you want family replies to land in
  • Whether you want text messaging turned on (a few of the steps need you, and we walk you through them)
