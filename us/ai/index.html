About this setup

Dominate AI gives you everything in Scale plus an AI chat widget on your website and an AI voice agent for inbound calls. Both answer from a knowledge base you'll build with us, so the AI never invents information about your studio.

This form takes around 30 to 45 minutes because the knowledge base needs detailed input from you. We'll have your account live within 7 to 10 business days of submission once the knowledge base is reviewed and approved.

📋
What you'll need before you start
  • Your studio logo (PNG or SVG with a transparent background works best)
  • Brand colour hex codes, or a screenshot we can match
  • The email address you want family replies to land in
  • An SMS number preference: local, toll-free, or porting an existing one
  • Studio profile, class schedule, pricing structure, and policies in your own words
  • At least 10 frequently asked questions with thorough answers
  • Voice agent rules: when it should engage, when it should escalate to a human
