The important distinction

What the platform calculates

All eligible tuition x class-count discount % = family term fee

At 8 classes, the platform applies a whole-stack discount to all 8 eligible class charges.

What it is not

It is not 95% off only the 8th, 9th, or 10th class as a separate line item. That wording makes the calculation sound different to how the system actually works.

How Neverland can explain this to families

Recommended wording

Multi-class discounts are based on the total number of eligible classes a student takes in the term. Families taking one to five classes pay normal class fees. From six classes onward, the discount increases, and from eight classes onward each extra class only adds a small amount to the term fee.

Short version

Our high-commitment multi-class discount rewards dancers who train across a larger weekly schedule, while keeping every class enrolment intentional.

Fee guide wording

Once a student reaches a multi-class discount tier, the tier percentage is applied to their eligible tuition total for the term.

Wording to avoid

Avoid: “Your 8th, 9th and 10th classes are 95% off.” That sounds like a line-item discount. Say the high-end tiers are structured so each additional eligible class adds only a small amount.
