thesis: StudioLAB Growth onboarding should capture public profile identifiers first, then guide the studio owner through secure OAuth connections inside the product without collecting passwords.
audience: studio owners and StudioLAB onboarding staff
one-line arc: identify the right public profiles, prepare the right account owner, connect each platform through the correct StudioLAB Growth area, then verify that the connection supports the intended workflow.

01. Cover, claim: Studio profile connections should feel like a guided setup video, proof object: branded setup journey.
02. Why this exists, claim: URLs identify accounts; OAuth authorises access, proof object: split-screen model.
03. No passwords, claim: the studio owner signs in directly with each platform, proof object: secure handoff diagram.
04. What to have ready, claim: most failed setup attempts are permission problems, proof object: pre-flight checklist.
05. Where connections happen, claim: three StudioLAB Growth areas cover the setup jobs, proof object: product navigation map.
06. Setup order, claim: connect Meta first, then Google, TikTok and YouTube as needed, proof object: timeline.
07. Facebook access, claim: Facebook needs Page control and extra lead access for ads, proof object: permission card.
08. Facebook click path, claim: leads, DMs and forms start in Settings > Integrations, proof object: annotated UI.
09. Facebook authorisation, claim: approve all requested permissions and select the Page, proof object: OAuth modal mock.
10. Facebook verification, claim: success means Page, messages and form mapping are visible, proof object: verification board.
11. Instagram access, claim: Business or Creator status drives what can connect, proof object: account readiness split.
12. Instagram click path, claim: social posting uses Social Planner, ads and DMs use the Meta integration, proof object: two-route map.
13. Instagram authorisation, claim: Direct Instagram Integration is the cleanest posting route, proof object: selection modal.
14. Instagram verification, claim: confirm the account, Page link and inbox status, proof object: check panel.
15. Google access, claim: GBP must be verified and managed by the connecting Google account, proof object: ownership card.
16. Google click path, claim: reviews and reputation connect from Settings, GBP posts use Social Planner or Reputation, proof object: navigation split.
17. Google authorisation, claim: select the exact business location and call tracking decision, proof object: location picker.
18. Google verification, claim: reputation sync and GBP posting require different checks, proof object: status board.
19. TikTok access, claim: posting, lead ads and comment automation are three different setup jobs, proof object: branching map.
20. TikTok click path, claim: posting starts in Social Planner; lead ads and automations start in Integrations, proof object: two-route UI.
21. TikTok authorisation, claim: lead ads require advertiser selection and form mapping, proof object: advertiser/form panel.
22. TikTok verification, claim: test the workflow that matches the package, proof object: test matrix.
23. YouTube access, claim: YouTube must be connected by the channel primary owner, proof object: owner gate.
24. YouTube click path, claim: connect YouTube only when publishing is included, proof object: Social Planner UI.
25. YouTube authorisation, claim: manager access cannot authorise third-party publishing, proof object: channel owner modal.
26. YouTube verification, claim: confirm the channel appears before scheduling, proof object: connected channel panel.
27. Internal handoff, claim: StudioLAB should verify the right capability, not just the green connection badge, proof object: operations checklist.
28. Production package, claim: one master deck plus five short videos is the cleanest onboarding system, proof object: delivery model.
29. Source notes, claim: every access requirement is source-backed and date stamped, proof object: source appendix.
